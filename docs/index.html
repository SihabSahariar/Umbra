How it works

Three things, fifteen times a second.

01

It reads where you're facing

Google's MediaPipe face tracker measures your head angle and where your eyes point. It runs entirely on your PC.

02

It waits a beat

Blinks and quick glances don't count. Only when you're really looking elsewhere for 0.7 s does Umbra act.

03

It covers every screen

A blur, your own picture, a GIF or video, or a solid colour, across every monitor. Clicks still pass through.

Features

Built for the five seconds
you're not looking.

Calibrated to you

A one-minute wizard has you look at your screen's edges, then away. Umbra learns your limits, wherever your camera sits.

F8

One key, anywhere

Toggle protection from any app, or pause it for up to an hour.

0.15s

Back before you notice

Look back and the cover is gone almost instantly.

Hide it your way

Lives in your tray

A crescent that turns amber while your screen is hidden. Right-click for everything.

Someone behind you?

Optionally hide your screen when a second face is looking at it, even while you are too.

Never locks you out

Clicks pass through the cover and it never steals focus. If the camera drops, your screen stays visible.

Privacy

Your camera feed
never leaves your PC.

0frames saved
0bytes uploaded
100%open source, GPL-3.0

Each frame is analysed in memory and thrown away. Umbra makes no network connections, and the camera is released the moment you turn protection off. Don't take our word for it: read the code.

Get started

Set up in about a minute.

  1. 01

    Install

    Run Umbra-Setup.exe. It installs just for you, with no admin prompt.

  2. 02

    Calibrate

    Look at the dot, the edges, then away. Press Space for each step.

  3. 03

    Forget about it

    Umbra sits in your tray. Press F8 whenever you want it off.
